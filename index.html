How to do a proper MEP take-off — Accurate quantities come from a clear process: confirm what you're measuring, ask about anything unclear before you count, and check every system before it goes to pricing.

  1. Register the drawings
  2. Review scope and specs
  3. Raise RFIs early
  4. Set up the take-off
  5. Measure and count
  6. Cross-check
  7. Close out RFIs and revisions
  8. Hand over

Architectural take-offs — Trade-by-trade quantities measured from plans, sections and elevations, with wastage allowances and clear notes, ready for pricing.

Most comfortable with Flooring Painting Ceiling Roofing

BOQ Preparation — A good BOQ starts from the drawings and specifications, not the price list. Each item is measured by trade or system, described clearly with its unit and quantity, and checked against the drawings before it's priced.

  1. Review drawings, specs and scope
  2. Measure quantities by trade or system
  3. Itemise with units, wastage and notes
  4. Cross-check, then roll up to a summary
